About Dr. Andrew L. Liu

I am an associate professor at Purdue University in Industrial Engineering. My research interests are energy and environmental markets; systems and policy modeling and analysis; decentralized algorithms of game theory and their applications in the smart grid; and optimization, complementarity problems, and variational inequalities, with applications to game theory and industrial organization.
