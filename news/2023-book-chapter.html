June 23, 2023

Our book chapter, 'Power Grid and Electrical Power System Security,' has been published in the Springer Handbook of Automation.

The book chapter, 'Power Grid and Electrical Power System Security,' co-authored with my former PhD student, Dr. Veronica R. Bosquezfoti, has been published in the Springer Handbook of Automation. The chapter provides an introduction to power systems, covering topics from long-term planning and short-term operations to reliability and cybersecurity aspects.
